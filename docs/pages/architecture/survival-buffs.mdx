# 20-Minute Milestone Buffs

Permanent speed and regeneration buffs to help close out long matches.

---

## The Buffs

When a match reaches the 20-minute mark, all remaining players receive permanent potion buffs to speed up pacing until the match ends:

- **Speed II:** Increased movement and sprinting speed across the world.
- **Regeneration I:** Steady passive health recovery.

Both buffs stay active until the match concludes.

# Match Flow

How a match progresses from lobby to victory.

---

## The 4 Match States

| State | Duration | What Happens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `LOBBY` | Unlimited | Players wait on the lobby platform. PvP, hunger, and damage are disabled. |
| `STARTING` | 10 seconds | Countdown plays on screen with sounds. Item roulette spins to pick the target block. |
| `IN_GAME` | Until win | Players scatter into fresh survival Overworld and Nether worlds. First pickup wins. |
| `ENDING` | 10 seconds | Winner celebration with fireworks. Players teleport back to lobby; match worlds delete. |

---

## Player Death & Respawn

- Players keep inventory on death inside match worlds.
- Respawn sends players back to their bed or match world spawn point.

---

## Party Matchmaking & 20s Ready Check

- **Party Match Initiation:** When a party leader starts a match with `/start` or via the Match Selector, an interactive ready-check menu opens for all party members.
- **20-Second Window:** Players click `[ Accept & Join ]` or `[ Decline ]`. If a player does not respond within 20 seconds, they are considered declined.
- **Automatic Start:** Once all players respond (or 20 seconds expire), racers who confirmed enter the 10-second countdown and roulette, while declining players stay safely in the lobby.

---

## Stopping a Match

- Admins can stop an active match anytime with `/ftb stop`.
- All players return to the lobby immediately with cleared inventories, and temporary worlds are deleted.

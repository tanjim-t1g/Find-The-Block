# Void Lobby

The default pre-game waiting area included with FindTheBlock.

---

## Platform Specs

- **World name:** `ftb_lobby` (created automatically if missing).
- **Size:** 100 &times; 100 bedrock platform centered around (0, 0).
- **Elevation:** Y = 100.
- **Spawn point:** X = 0.5, Y = 101.0, Z = 0.5.

---

## Void Boundary Protection

- If a player falls or jumps off the platform, they are caught at **Y = 90**.
- The player is instantly teleported back to the center spawn.
- No fall damage is dealt, and no death screen is triggered.

---

## Lobby Features & Controls

- **Match Selector Compass:** Given automatically in hotbar slot 4. Right-click to browse active matches, join/spectate mid-game, or access the Party Finder.
- **In-World Leaderboards:** Admins can spawn floating hologram leaderboards with `/ftb leaderboard spawn [wins|time]` anywhere on the platform.
- **Clean Inventories:** Returning to the lobby clears survival gear so players never carry tools or gathered items into the lobby area.

---

## Lobby Rules

- PvP is disabled.
- Hunger does not drain.
- Weather is locked to clear daylight.
- Blocks cannot be broken or placed without bypass permission.

# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions and troubleshooting topics about FindTheBlock.

---

## Can players enter the Nether?

Yes. The Overworld and Nether are paired with the identical seed. Players can build and light Nether portals to gather Nether items like Blaze Rods, Nether Quartz, or Ancient Debris.

---

## Can players enter the End?

Yes. The End dimension is loaded on-demand per match only if a player enters an End portal. If no one enters the End, it is not loaded to save server resources. When the match ends, all dimensions (Overworld, Nether, and End) are deleted automatically.

---

## What happens if a player disconnects?

Players have a 60-second window to reconnect and resume where they left off. If they do not reconnect within 60 seconds, their spot is forfeited and they are treated as a late joiner upon rejoining.

---

## Can players join an active match?

Yes. When a player logs in during an active round, they can use `/join` to jump into the race or `/spectate` to watch in spectator flight mode.

---

## How do I reset all leaderboard stats?

Stop your server, delete the statistics storage file inside `plugins/FindTheBlock/` (e.g. `stats.yml`), and restart the server.

---

## Why are placeholders displaying raw text?

Make sure you have PlaceholderAPI installed on your Paper server and run `/papi reload`.

# FindTheBlock

A fast item-rush minigame for Paper 1.21+ servers. Players race in a fresh world to find or craft a randomly chosen item.

---

## The Game in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Lobby:** Players gather in the lobby. Party leader or admin starts with `/start`.
2. **Roulette:** A 10-second countdown plays, and an item roulette spins on screen to pick a target block.
3. **The Race:** Players scatter into a fresh survival world with a linked Nether. First person to pick up or craft the target item wins!
